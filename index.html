Is it Recycling?

Pittsburgh's bi-weekly recycling, answered instantly.

Recycling in Pittsburgh and the surrounding Allegheny County municipalities is picked up every other week, and nobody can keep track. This app answers one question the moment you open it: is this recycling week, and when is your next pickup?

  • One-time setup. Use your location once, or type your address, and the app finds your municipality and schedule.
  • Works offline. After setup everything runs on your phone. No accounts, no tracking.
  • Premium ($1.99, one time). Ask Siri "is it recycling week?" and get a reminder the evening before recycling goes out.

Covers the City of Pittsburgh and 29 surrounding municipalities. Schedules are checked against each municipality's official calendar.

Support

Questions, a wrong pickup day, or a municipality you'd like added: email recycling@precipice.design.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. The short version: your location is used once, on your phone, to find your schedule, and nothing about you is collected or shared.
