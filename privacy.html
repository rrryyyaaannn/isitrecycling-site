Privacy Policy

Is it Recycling? · Last updated September 22, 2026

Is it Recycling? is designed to work without knowing who you are. This page explains exactly what the app does with information on your device.

Location

During setup the app asks to use your location once, to work out which municipality you live in and your pickup schedule. This happens on your phone. You can decline and type an address instead. The app does not track your location afterwards and never runs in the background.

Your address

The street address you enter or that is derived from your location is stored only on your phone, so the app can show it in Settings and look up your schedule. It is not sent to us. There is no account and no server operated by us.

City of Pittsburgh addresses

If your address is inside the City of Pittsburgh, the app sends your coordinates to the City of Pittsburgh's public map service (ArcGIS) to look up your collection day and recycling zone. This is the same public data the city publishes on its own website. We do not receive or store the request.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software. We cannot see who uses the app or where they live.

Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and keeps the purchase record; we never see your payment details.

Siri and notifications

Siri requests and reminder notifications are processed on your device by iOS. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
